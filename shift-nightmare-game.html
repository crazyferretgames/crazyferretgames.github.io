FIELD GUIDE

Night shift survival notes · Harlan Textile Mill

THE FOREMAN Both floors

Emmett Voss ran the night shift. After the machine accident on loom 6 he chained the gate and never let the shift end. He still walks the mill dragging his chain.

Senses: Hears footsteps (sprinting is loudest, crouching nearly silent) and hears you breathing in a locker. Sees in a cone: sharp straight ahead, weak at the edges, blind behind. Lit areas and your flashlight give you away; crouched behind crates or barrels you can vanish. The eye at the top of the screen fills as he notices you.

Counter: Crouch and keep to the dark. Break his line of sight, hide in a locker and hold SPACE while he is close. Stairs and vents work for you too. He cannot go into the cellar.

THE LURKER Cellar, then the whole mill after the last key

Whatever Harlan sealed below the boiler room after the accident. Tall, silent, and starving for the dark. Its white eyes are the only thing you will ever see of it.

Behavior: Stalks slowly and freezes while you watch it, then creeps in when you look away. A distant growl means it has found you. A loud growl means it is right behind you and about to strike.

Counter: Turn and shine your flashlight on it. Hold the light and it shrieks and flees, staying gone for a while. Keep batteries in reserve and never leave your light off down there. Lockers keep you safe.

FALSE LOCKERS Anywhere

Some lockers are not empty. Something wearing the shape of a locker waits for a worker to climb inside.

Tells: Eyes glow behind the slats and you can hear slow breathing when you get close. Real lockers are silent.

Counter: Never open one that breathes. If you go inside a false locker, it is over. Use a different locker.

HAZARDS

Tripwires and bear traps: jump over them with SPACE. Setting one off tells the Foreman where you are.

Security cameras: stay out of the beam of light. Walls block it and it sweeps back and forth, so time your dash. Staying in it too long sounds the alarm.

Vents: shortcuts for the Foreman and for you (E). Listen for scraping in the walls.

LORE

1987: Harlan Textile expands the night shift. Oct 3, 1993: the loom 6 accident. Forty-one clocked in and thirty-eight clocked out. The county was told three. The mill closes but a night watchman is hired every year. This year it is you. Read the notes beside the bodies to piece the rest together.
